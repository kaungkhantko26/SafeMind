      Safety notice

      Never share an OTP or recovery code

      Legitimate support teams do not need your private verification code. End the conversation and verify through the official app.

      Trending scam

      Account closure pressure

      Messages threatening immediate account suspension are commonly used to steal credentials.

      Community reports

      Impersonation remains active

      Verify bank, delivery, marketplace, and support contacts through independently found channels.

      Threat intelligence

      Short links hide destinations

      Do not open shortened links from unexpected senders. Check the destination first.

      Recovery guide

      Act quickly after payment

      Contact your financial provider, preserve evidence, change exposed credentials, and report the incident.
